📌 Essential Circular Instructions Before Applying

  • Participation in Panchmukhi Shiksha is compulsory for every UG/PG student. Group 4 (Moral Education) is compulsory for all — in addition, you pick one activity each from Groups 1, 2, and 3, and may apply to a maximum of two in total.
  • Moral Education carries 1 credit. Of your chosen activities, your best one earns 1 credit and the other is graded only.
  • Repeating an activity from a previous year is allowed, but it will only be graded, not credited again.
  • Grades from these activities are added to your marksheet and affect your overall result.
  • PG students: 1st/2nd semester activities are credited; 3rd/4th semester (final year) activities are graded only.
  • Application deadline as per circular: on or before 18 January 2026 (or as indicated on your department notice board).
